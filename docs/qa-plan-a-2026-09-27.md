## Critical findings

1. **Opening deductions fail (TC-BOQ-002–004):** With a controlled 4.00 m wall at 2.80 m height, the material quantity stays at 22.40 m² after adding a 0.90 × 2.10 m door, a 1.20 × 1.20 m window, or both. The document expects deductions of 1.89 and 1.44 m² and a 7.87 m² net single-face area. `calculateBudget` sums applied wall faces and does not subtract openings. The BOQ also does not expose the expected 11.20 m² single-face gross measure.

2. **Material click differs from the document (TC-MAT-004):** The default workflow selects a material on click, then requires a separate Apply button. Instant Apply exists only for some categories. The test case expects card click to commit directly. This may be a deliberate UX choice, but it fails the written Expected Result.

3. **Wall thickness uses a field (TC-2D-005):** There is no drag handle for thickness in 2D. A numeric input and preset buttons work as an alternative, but the documented drag step cannot be performed.

4. **Branch coverage:** The document targets “Post Phase 2 + Focus Mode”; this branch predates its Walk Mode, expanded Focus Mode, and SCG sample catalog. Those cases are BLOCKED here. The newer `main` branch has more of these features, but this run intentionally used the branch requested by the user.
